Maple Wood
Maple is a hardwood known for its strength, fine texture, and uniform appearance. It is popular in furniture, flooring, and musical instruments.

Maple comes from trees of the genus Acer, native primarily to North America and parts of Europe and Asia. There are several types, including hard maple (sugar maple) and soft maple (red and silver maple).

Construction and Workability

  • Hard and dense, especially sugar maple
  • Moderate to high difficulty in cutting, shaping, and sanding
  • Excellent for cabinetry, flooring, and furniture components
  • Accepts stains and finishes well, though hard maple can be challenging to stain evenly

Durability

  • Very durable and resistant to wear
  • Hard maple is highly resistant to dents and scratches
  • Soft maple less resistant but easier to work

Pliability

  • Moderately flexible when steamed or bent
  • Can be used for curved furniture and instrument parts

Color and Appearance

  • Heartwood: light reddish-brown to creamy white
  • Uniform, fine, and straight grain patterns
  • Occasional birdseye or curly maple patterns prized for decorative uses

Cost

  • Moderate; hard maple more expensive than soft maple
  • Decorative patterns like birdseye maple can be significantly higher

Common Uses

  • Furniture and cabinetry
  • Flooring and paneling
  • Musical instruments (violins, guitars, pianos)
  • Cutting boards and kitchen utensils
  • Sports equipment (bowling pins, baseball bats)

Origin

  • Native to North America, Europe, and Asia
  • Sugar maple (Acer saccharum) – Northeastern US and Canada
  • Red maple (Acer rubrum) – Eastern US
  • Silver maple (Acer saccharinum) – Eastern and Central US

Other Characteristics

  • Strong impact resistance, especially hard maple
  • Good dimensional stability when properly dried
  • Can be treated or finished for long-lasting performance
  • Decorative patterns like curly or birdseye are highly valued
Maple is a versatile hardwood prized for both functional and decorative uses, combining strength, durability, and aesthetic appeal.
